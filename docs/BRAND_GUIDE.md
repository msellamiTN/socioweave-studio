# SocioWeave Brand Guide

## 1. Naming architecture

- **SOCIOWEAVE** — master brand.
- **SocioWeave Studio** — product and research workspace.
- **SocioDec Engine** — the formal strategy execution engine within the Studio.
- **SocioWeave Research** — research, datasets, benchmarks and reproducibility programme.

Use the uppercase form **SOCIOWEAVE** for the brand lockup and title treatments. Use **SocioWeave** in prose and **socioweave-studio** for repository and package identifiers.

## 2. Name definition

**Socio** refers to the social and organisational processes used to structure group work: specialisation, independence, dissent, deliberation, mediation, judgement and collective decisions.

**Weave** expresses how agents, roles, information visibility, protocol steps, decision rules and task dependencies are interconnected into a coherent decomposition process.

Together, **SocioWeave** means the structured weaving of agent collaboration into an auditable problem-decomposition process.

## 3. Positioning

A research-oriented studio for designing, executing, inspecting and evaluating social and organisational strategies for LLM-based multi-agent prompt decomposition.

### Tagline

**From complex prompts to collaborative intelligence.**

Supporting phrase: **Research with structure. Collaboration with purpose.**

## 4. Logo concept

The symbol is a network weave:

- Outer nodes represent agents and social roles.
- Connecting strokes represent communication and visibility.
- The central teal node represents coordination and decision convergence.
- Interlaced paths represent dependencies and protocol structure.

The mark is a conceptual identity, not a diagram of one specific paper strategy.

## 6. Typography

Preferred UI fonts:
- Inter
- fallback: system-ui, Segoe UI, Arial, sans-serif

Use strong, concise headings; body copy should remain highly readable. Avoid all-caps for long paragraphs.

## 8. Logo usage

- Prefer the full wordmark on README, landing pages and presentations.
- Use the square symbol for favicon, app icon and compact navigation.
- Preserve clear space equal to at least the central-node diameter.
- Do not stretch, rotate, recolour arbitrarily, add shadows, or place the mark on low-contrast backgrounds.
- Do not separate the symbol's inner network paths.

## 9. Voice and writing

The brand should feel:
- Scientific, not speculative.
- Collaborative, not anthropomorphic.
- Precise, not overclaiming.
- Transparent about experimental status and limitations.

Prefer “evaluated”, “measured” and “validated” only when evidence exists. Distinguish paper-defined mechanisms, candidate strategies and implementation extensions.

## 10. Product naming examples

- SocioWeave Studio — the application.
- SocioDec Engine — strategy runtime.
- Strategy Catalogue — S1–S13 and candidate strategies.
- Decomposition Graph — DAG workspace.
- Experiment Lab — benchmark campaigns and metrics.
- Research Ledger — provenance and reproducibility records.
